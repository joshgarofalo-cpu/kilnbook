KKilnbook

Every piece, from the shelf to pickup.

A simple phone app for a paint-your-own pottery studio. It tracks bisque inventory and every customer's piece until they take it home.

Try this

  1. Tap New painter, add a Dino figurine, and save. Watch for the low-stock alert.
  2. Open Pickups and load the waiting pieces into the kiln.
  3. Unload the kiln. Each customer gets a "ready for pickup" text.
  4. Back on Home, nudge the two people who forgot their pieces last month.
